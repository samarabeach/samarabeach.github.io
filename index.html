01About

Samara Beach is an economic researcher and hobbyist writer based in Washington, D.C. Having grown up in the Tampa Bay area and rural southeastern Illinois, she graduated from Amherst College in 2022 with a Bachelor of Arts magna cum laude in economics and mathematics. She worked as a research assistant for the Federal Reserve Board of Governors from 2022 to 2025.
